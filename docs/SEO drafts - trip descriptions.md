## The Great Migration (8 days)

Follow the wildebeest migration on an eight-day safari through Tanzania's northern circuit. After a welcome and safari briefing in Arusha, start in Tarangire National Park, known for its huge elephant herds and ancient baobabs. Then head into the Serengeti for full days following the herds across the central plains and watching predators in action, before travelling north for the chance to see the dramatic Mara River crossings, where wildebeest and zebras brave crocodile-filled waters.

On the way back, stop at Olduvai Gorge, an important archaeological site, and spend a final game drive on the floor of the Ngorongoro Crater, home to rhinos, lions and hippos. Wildlife movements and river crossings vary with the season, so we plan your route around your travel dates.

## Ngorongoro Crater Safari (4 days)

A captivating four-day safari into the heart of the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most wildlife-dense areas in Africa. After arriving at Kilimanjaro International Airport and a relaxing first night in Arusha, drive up to the crater highlands, stopping at scenic viewpoints, and spend the evening at a lodge or camp near the crater rim.

Day three is the highlight: a full-day game drive on the crater floor, looking for lions, elephants, zebras and the rare black rhino, with a picnic lunch by the hippo pool. Perfect for wildlife lovers who want an unforgettable taste of Tanzania's wilderness in just four days.

## Materuni Waterfall Day Trip

The Materuni Waterfall is a picturesque cascade in the lush foothills of Mount Kilimanjaro, and this day trip pairs it with an authentic cultural experience in a traditional Chagga village. From Moshi or Arusha, drive to Materuni village past coffee plantations, then take a short guided hike through banana and coffee farms to the waterfall, which drops about 70 metres.

Swim in the cool natural pool or relax and take in the views, then join a Chagga family to pick, roast, grind and brew coffee the traditional way, and enjoy a freshly made cup. Ideal for travellers who want nature, culture and a little adventure in one day.

## Ngorongoro Crater Day Trip

Spend a full day in the Ngorongoro Crater, one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations, without needing an overnight stay. You travel in a 4x4 safari vehicle with a professional English-speaking guide, and park entry fees, a packed lunch and bottled water are included.

The crater floor is home to lions, elephants, zebras, hippos and the rare black rhino. You can visit in the dry season, from June to October, or the green wet season, from November to May.

## Kendwa Beach Escape (6 days)

Enjoy the ultimate Zanzibar holiday: six days and five nights combining history, beach and ocean adventures. Start in Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, then head north to Kendwa Beach, known for its soft white sand, turquoise water and lively atmosphere.

Go snorkelling or diving at Mnemba Atoll to discover Zanzibar's vibrant marine life, visit Nungwi village for local culture and crafts, and end your days with Zanzibar's beautiful sunsets. Accommodation for all five nights, in Stone Town and Kendwa, is included.
